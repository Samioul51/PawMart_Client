PawMart - Pet Adoption & Supply Portal
Live Site: PawMart Live Site
Features

- Comprehensive Pet Adoption Platform - Users can browse, filter, and adopt pets from various categories including dogs, cats, and other animals. Pet owners and breeders can easily list pets for adoption with detailed information and images.
- Pet Supplies Marketplace - A complete marketplace for pet-related products including food, toys, accessories, and care products. Shop owners can add their products with pricing, descriptions, and locations for local buyers to purchase.
- Personalized Dashboard Management - Registered users have access to dedicated "My Listings" and "My Orders" pages where they can manage their posted pets/products, update information, delete listings, and track all their adoption requests or purchases in one place.
- PDF Order Reports - Download detailed PDF reports of all your orders with a single click. The report includes complete order information such as product names, quantities, prices, addresses, and dates in a professional tabular format.
- Secure Authentication & User Experience - Firebase-powered authentication with both email-password and Google login options. Includes form validation, private route protection, dynamic page titles, dark/light mode toggle, and smooth animations using Framer Motion for an enhanced user experience.
